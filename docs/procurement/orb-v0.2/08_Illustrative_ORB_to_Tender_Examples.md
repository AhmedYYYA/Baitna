# BAITNA — Illustrative ORB-to-Tender Examples
## أمثلة شكلية غير حقيقية، للاختبار فقط

### Example A — Abu Dhabi private villa, concept design stage
**ORB selection (synthetic)**: Emirate Abu Dhabi; project new_build; maturity concept; desired majlis=yes; floor-area indication 550m²; budget not disclosed; target design-and-supervision consultant.  
**Recommended instrument**: **RFP** rather than Firm RFQ, because technical methodology, concept and design verification are still to be developed.  
**Generated response matrix**: consultant experience and qualifications, work plan/design phases, preliminary timeline, clear deliverables (design, tender drawings, engineering coordination), price structure and exclusions.  
**Local overlay**: ADB-PV-01 flagged for technical review of private villa rules and any applicable annex/majlis requirements. The platform **does not** assert a numeric setback, height or permissible ratio based on this illustrative ORB.  
**Not generated**: approved building permits, final contract, approved SOW and IMS baseline.

### Example B — Ajman renovation with quantified BOQ
**ORB selection (synthetic)**: Emirate Ajman; project renovation; drawings and line-by-line finish BOQ available; owner selects three comparable contractors; pricing basis `firm_based_on_defined_scope`; work unit and acceptance criteria present.  
**Recommended instrument**: **RFQ** with a line-priced BOQ, alternatives policy, tax treatment, schedule/defects obligations, and a mandatory deviations register.  
**Local overlay**: AJM-BP-01 flags professional confirmation whether renovation needs a permit and which plans or approvals apply to this particular scope; do not assume a new-building permit always applies.  
**Comparison**: normalize line totals, inclusions and exclusions, record technical compliance, and recommend a supplier. This does not legally execute a contract.  
**After selection**: produce a DRAFT Contract, DRAFT SOW, DRAFT WBS/BOQ and DRAFT IMS, each under separate authorization and version control.

### Example C — Umm Al Quwain, uncertain authority details
**ORB selection (synthetic)**: UAQ, extension project, unknown local service and jurisdiction.  
**Allowed**: owner can create draft RFI and obtain high-level market information without claiming that a local permit condition is verified.  
**Blocked**: automatically adding an allegedly mandatory UAQ clause or publishing a claimed code-compliant RFP/RFQ.
**Resolution**: confirm primary official municipality service text, date and authority; technical/legal reviewer approves RulePack before issuing locally-specific legal clauses.

### Expected acceptance
- Each generated annex records ORB snapshot and version, rulepack source version and scope-maturity assumptions.
- Sensitive owner contact/identity, exact plot documentation and hidden budget remain excluded from supplier-facing exports.
- If an RFP's 4 criterion weights do not sum to 100%, pre-issue validation fails.
- No supplier can access a competitor's bid.
